# Garden assets

Selected models and textures from Quaternius Stylized Nature MegaKit (Standard).
License: CC0 1.0. Original license: `nature/License_Standard.txt`.
All required glTF, binary and texture files are included here; no developer paths are used.

`garden-landscape-v2.png` is the active GPT Image-generated backdrop.
Two trees are permanent decoration at zero completed visits. Further plants,
paths, the bench and pond appear in five deterministic growth stages.
Only completed personal visits affect growth; there are no preview controls.
Models load on entering the garden and repeated instances reuse geometry and textures.
